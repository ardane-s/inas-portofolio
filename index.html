Who I Am?

I am an F&B Service team member with on-call banquet experience at Bali Nusa Dua Convention Center and Paradisus by Meliá Bali. My work includes event service, wedding set-menu placements and seating-chart preparation.

At KAFE during Bali Spirit Festival 2026, I supported a busy service operation, recording more than 1,500 transactions in ESB Lite and engaging with over 300 guests each day.

I bring a foundation in Food & Beverage Service, a background in Software Engineering, and an intermediate level of English. I value clear communication, accurate service and dependable teamwork.

Studied Food & Beverage Service at Mediterranean Bali Ubud from 2025 to 2026.

Completed vocational studies in Software Engineering at SMK Negeri 1 Gianyar from 2020 to 2023 before focusing on hospitality.

This combination of study gives me a service-focused foundation and familiarity with digital tools used in day-to-day operations.

I work across banquet and event settings, from convention service to resort functions and a high-volume festival café.

I am comfortable communicating in Bahasa Indonesia (native) and English (intermediate).
