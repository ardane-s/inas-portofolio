Who I Am?

Hello, I am Ardan, an aspiring Food & Beverage Service professional with a passion for creating memorable guest experiences through genuine hospitality, attention to detail, and continuous learning.

Through every event, guest interaction, and service shift, I have continued to develop my communication, teamwork, adaptability, and problem-solving skills.

Alongside my studies at Mediterranean Bali Ubud, I have gained hands-on experience in banquet operations, hospitality events, and high-volume service environments. These experiences have strengthened my ability to work under pressure, collaborate effectively, and maintain consistent service standards.

I believe that exceptional hospitality goes beyond delivering service. It is about professionalism, discipline, attention to detail, and a genuine commitment to making every guest feel welcomed, valued, and remembered.

Currently pursuing studies at Mediterranean Bali Ubud, specializing in Hotel & Restaurant Operations with an emphasis on Food & Beverage Service.

My coursework balances intensive practical training in banquet setups, table service standards, and beverage knowledge with hospitality management principles, guest communication, and operational hygiene.

Hands-on practical shifts at high-caliber events have reinforced the importance of situational awareness, calm coordination under pressure, and gracious hospitality in multi-cultural settings.

Beyond the service floor, I bring a unique dual background in technology and digital workflows. I believe modern hospitality thrives when human warmth is complemented by thoughtful digital tools and efficient operational systems.

My personal interests include Balinese culture and temple arts, coffee brewing techniques, photography, and language learning to connect meaningfully with global travelers visiting Bali.
